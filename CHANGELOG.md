# Changelog

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-01)

- A dashboard (plugin view `mind`, AstrBot 4.24.2+): what she sent today and what is unanswered, her recent
  thoughts and where each went (said, kept, let go) and why, what she keeps for later, what she sent and whether
  it was answered; think once (a dry run, like /tov_think), let go of the kept thoughts.
